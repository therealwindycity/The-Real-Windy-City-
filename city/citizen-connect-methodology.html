DATA SCOPE & LIMITATIONS

Counts from different layers must stay separate.

Citizen Connect is a third-party research tool. This methodology page sets interpretation limits; it does not certify any specific export or claim that all source categories are present.

Incidents and Cases are different layers

An incident record and a case record may describe connected activity, but they are not interchangeable. Adding the two totals does not produce a count of unique crimes, unique events, or arrests. Never merge records by name, location, or date alone.

Coverage and withheld categories

Missing, suppressed, withheld, delayed, or differently categorized records affect what can be observed. A dashboard count describes the included dataset and date range, not all activity. State the source, filters, extraction date, and known omissions alongside any reported total.

Dates describe different events

Publication date, incident date, arrest date, and court disposition date are distinct. They should not be substituted for one another or treated as a single timeline without documented linkage.

Geographic limits

Mapped locations may be generalized, incomplete, or assigned for display. Generated coordinates from another application are not verified locations and must never enter a verified geographic analysis. Preserve the original location fields and label any geocoding method and uncertainty.

Reproducibility rules

  • Keep source exports immutable and retain retrieval date, URL, schema/version, and file hash.
  • Document filters and transformations; report counts before and after joins.
  • Use source-provided stable IDs for joins only when their semantics support the relationship.
  • Do not infer guilt, causation, or unique-person counts from a map or aggregate table.
  • Record corrections and update every affected chart and public summary.

Current archive status

No Citizen Connect incident/case dataset or reproducible export is included in this repository’s checked-in public catalog. This page therefore publishes methodology only, not incident counts or mapped findings.

Keep datasets separate.

Do not merge Citizen Connect layers with meeting transcripts or arrest records by names, dates, or proximity.

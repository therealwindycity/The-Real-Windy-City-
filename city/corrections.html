How to request a correction

  1. Identify the page and exact passage (include a timestamp for a transcript).
  2. Link the primary record or recording and describe what it supports.
  3. Use the repository issue tracker or contact the publisher. Do not include private or sensitive personal information.

Accepted corrections should include: the original wording; the corrected wording; supporting evidence; date corrected; and every linked summary or related page reviewed for updates.

Source errors

An error in a government source is part of the historical record. Preserve the source as published, identify the relevant page or passage, and add an annotation citing the correction evidence. Do not silently “repair” the source copy or represent the correction as the publisher’s own update unless the publisher has issued one.

Potential source inconsistencies that have not been independently checked are described as unresolved questions, not published as established corrections.

# Cheyenne, Wyoming — City Council & Committee Meeting Transcripts (2026)

**Verbatim transcripts of every 2026 public meeting of the Cheyenne City Council, its Finance and Public Services committees, council work sessions, the Planning Commission, Board of Adjustment, Historic Preservation Board, and Urban Renewal Authority** — derived from the city's official YouTube videos and published here as searchable text so residents, journalists, researchers, and AI tools can quote and analyze exactly what was said.

Why this exists: the City of Cheyenne posts meeting *videos* to YouTube and minutes (summaries) to its website, but no full-text record. Video is not searchable, not quotable, and invisible to search engines. This archive closes that gap — one Markdown file per meeting, each with `[HH:MM:SS]` timestamps so any passage can be checked against the video in seconds.

## About this archive

- **Source:** English captions on the City of Cheyenne's official YouTube channel (`@TheCityofCheyenne`). Human-uploaded subtitles are used where present; otherwise YouTube's auto-generated captions.
- **Catalog:** [`meetings.json`](meetings.json) lists every video ID, date, body, and file path — machine-readable for researchers and AI tools.
- **Not included:** closed-door executive sessions (e.g. the early-2026 fire-negotiation specials), which have no public video, and CTAC "Fireside Chat" interviews, which are not public meetings. Also missing: the 2026-06-18 Board of Adjustment video has no caption track on YouTube, so no transcript could be produced unless the city adds one.

## Accuracy & fair use

Auto-generated captions mis-transcribe words and names. Timestamps let you verify any passage against the video. These are public meetings of a Wyoming local government; transcripts are published as a public-record research archive. Spot an error the captions made that matters? [Open an issue](../../issues) with the timestamp and video link.
